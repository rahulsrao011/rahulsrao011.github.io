## About Me
Currently, I work as a software engineer at [Google ↗](https://google.com){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"}. Before that, I worked as a software engineer intern at [Capital One ↗](https://www.capitalone.com){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"}. I also have experience working at various startups and research labs.\
I graduated from the [University of Wisconsin-Madison (UW-Madison) ↗](https://www.wisc.edu){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"} with an undergraduate degree in computer science and mathematics (double major). During my time attending UW-Madison, I was a coordinator at the [Undergraduate Projects Lab (UPL) ↗](https://upl.cs.wisc.edu/){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"}.\
My academic interests include linear algebra (all sub-fields included like numerical, randomized, sparse, etc.), GPU programming (specifically writing efficient CUDA kernels), and systems programming (operating systems, computer networks, computer architecture, etc.). I've also scored well on several math competitions including [Putnam ↗](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Putnam_Competition){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"} (top 770 of ~4000 nationally), 2x [AIME ↗](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Invitational_Mathematics_Examination){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"} qualifier, and [USAMTS ↗](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_of_America_Mathematical_Talent_Search){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"} honorable mention.\
Outside of work, I like to play sports (like ultimate frisbee, table tennis, and badminton), social deduction + strategy games (like [Coup ↗](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coup_(card_game)){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"}, [Avalon (aka The Resistance) ↗](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Resistance_(game)){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"}, and chess), and hike + explore nature.
